# The template catalogue

Written 24 September 2026. 127 templates worth having, 39 of them first.

## Layouts and presets

A template is two things. Underneath is the **layout**: how the page works, whether it takes a
booking or a bag. On top is the **preset**: name, headline, section order, words, palette, sample
content, photo treatment. Eight layouts carry every preset in this list.

A virtual assistant and a video editor share a layout and share nothing a visitor can see.
